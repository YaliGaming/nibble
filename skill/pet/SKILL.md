The user typed `/pet` to turn their Nibble desktop pet on or off.

Nibble's own hook normally answers this command before it ever reaches you. You
are reading this, so that hook is not installed. Do not run anything. Tell the
user, in one short sentence, to open Nibble, go to its settings and press
Connect, after which `/pet` will work.
